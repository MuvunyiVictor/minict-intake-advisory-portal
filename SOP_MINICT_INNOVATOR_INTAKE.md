# REPUBLIC OF RWANDA — MINISTRY OF ICT AND INNOVATION (MINICT)
## STANDARD OPERATING PROCEDURE (SOP)
### Innovator Intake, Directed Concept Vetting, and Inter-Ministerial Triaging

* **Document Code:** MINICT-SOP-IET-2026-01
* **Effective Date:** October 2026
* **Issuing Authority:** Office of the Permanent Secretary
* **Target Audience:** Technical Advisors, Innovation Officers, Legal Specialists, and Administrative Staff

---

### 1. Purpose & Objective
This SOP establishes a standardized, equitable, and data-driven workflow for receiving, vetting, triaging, and acting upon reachouts, support requests, and MoU proposals submitted to the Ministry of ICT and Innovation.

### 2. Scope
Applies to all inbound requests from:
* Commercial Tech Startups & SMEs
* Individual Innovators and Independent Developers
* Academic and Research Institutions
* Development Partners and NGOs seeking digital implementation

---

#### Stage 1: Public Submission & Automated Gap Analysis
1. All applicants must submit their request through the portal at `https://www.minict.gov.rw/portal` or `https://innovaterwanda.rw/portal`.
2. The portal enforces completion of the **Directed Concept Note**, requiring technical architecture details, IP filing status, NCSA data protection registration, and target sector selection.
3. The automated engine evaluates foundational readiness:
   - **Score < 50%:** Issues an automated advisory checklist outlining missing prerequisites. The applicant is advised to rectify gaps before requesting an in-person meeting.
   - **Score 50% - 74%:** Logged with status *Advised - Pending Prerequisites*.
   - **Score ≥ 75%:** Logged with status *Ready for Technical Review*.

#### Stage 2: Central Logging & Reference Assignment
1. Every application receives an immutable timestamp and sequential tracking code: `MINICT-YYYY-NNN`.
2. The applicant receives an on-screen confirmation and printable PDF summary.

#### Stage 3: Technical Officer Triaging Matrix
Upon receipt in the MINICT Internal Dashboard, the Innovation & Emerging Tech division applies the following rules:

| Sector Vertical | Mandatory Prerequisite | Action / Referral Protocol |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **HealthTech** | MoH Digital Health Working Group approval & Rwanda FDA clearance | Issue formal referral letter to MoH e-Health lead; hold MINICT MoU until clinical clearance is secured. |
| **EdTech** | Rwanda Basic Education Board (REB) curriculum alignment | Route concept to REB ICT in Education unit for pedagogical evaluation. |
| **FinTech** | BNR Regulatory Sandbox entry or Payment Services license | Coordinate joint session with BNR Innovation team and RSwitch for eKash switch testing. |
| **General AI / GovTech** | NCSA Data Protection compliance & RDB IP registration | Assign to MINICT Emerging Tech Technical Officer for technical feasibility assessment. |

#### Stage 4: Execution of Memoranda of Understanding (MoU)
A formal MINICT MoU will only be drafted if:
1. The applicant possesses a registered legal entity with RDB.
2. Intellectual property registration or application has been submitted to RDB IP.
3. A functional MVP or pilot metrics have been demonstrated.
4. The line ministry has issued written no-objection or joint endorsement.

---

### 4. Roles and Responsibilities

* **Permanent Secretary (PS):** Approves strategic escalations, oversees ecosystem trends via the analytics dashboard, and signs finalized MoUs.
* **Head of Innovation & Emerging Technologies:** Oversees weekly triaging, monitors sector bottlenecks, and initiates policy training interventions.
* **Assigned Technical Officer (e.g. Victor Muvunyi):** Conducts in-depth architectural assessments and coordinates testing with technical partners.
* **Legal Affairs Directorate:** Drafts and validates formal MoUs once technical clearance is satisfied.
